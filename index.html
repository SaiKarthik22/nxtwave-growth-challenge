          FAQ
          
Questions, answered.

        

        

          

            Do I need to know AI or machine learning?+
            
No. If you can write a basic function in any language, you can follow along. We use a 3-line helper so you focus on building, not maths.

          

          

            What do I need on the day?+
            
A laptop, Chrome and a stable internet connection. We send 10-minute setup steps on WhatsApp the day before, so you start building from minute one.

          

          

            Is it really free?+
            
Yes. No payment, no card, no hidden upsell during the build. The workshop is free and the project is yours.

          

          

            Can non-CSE branches join?+
            
Absolutely. ECE, EEE, Mech and Civil students build the same project. The skill is prompting and shipping, not your branch.

          

          

            Will I get a certificate?+
            
Yes. Submit your deployed project link at the end of the session and your certificate is emailed to you.

          

          

            How do referral rewards work?+
            
After registering you get a personal link. Every friend who registers through it counts: 1 unlocks the Prompt Pack, 3 unlock the Starter Repo + certificate, 5 get your project reviewed live. Track it in the Referral Hub.
