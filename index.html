          Before you sign up
          
What students usually ask

        

        

          

            I've never studied AI. Will I keep up?+
            
Yes. Basic programming in any language is enough. A three-line helper makes the AI call for you, so the hour goes into building your app and there's no maths to learn first.

          

          

            What should I have ready on Sunday?+
            
A laptop with Chrome and steady internet. The day before, we WhatsApp you a setup guide that takes about 10 minutes, so nobody spends the session installing things.

          

          

            Is there a fee or a catch?+
            
No fee and no card details. Nobody pitches a paid course while you're building, and the app and its code stay yours after the session.

          

          

            I'm in ECE, EEE, Mech or Civil. Should I still come?+
            
Please do. The skills here are writing clear prompts and putting an app online, and they work the same in every branch. Everyone builds the same apps side by side.

          

          

            Can I ask my doubts in Telugu?+
            
Yes. Mentors in the chat reply in Telugu or English, whichever you write in.

          

          

            How do I get the certificate?+
            
At the end of the session, submit the public link of your deployed app. Your certificate of completion then arrives by email.

          

          

            Is there a recording?+
            
Yes. Everyone registered gets the recording and the code templates afterwards. Mentor help in the chat only happens during the live hour, so join live if you can.

          

          

            What do I get for inviting friends?+
            
Signing up gives you a personal link. One friend through it unlocks the AI Prompt Pack, three unlock the Starter Repo + Early-Bird Certificate, and five get your app reviewed live by the instructor. Check your count any time in the Referral Hub.
